Rules used for demonstration

Quick scalp: $1–$2 move; requires clean M1, confirmed M5, trend alignment, ATR $1.2–$3.5, spread ≤$0.25.
Selective scalp: $3–$5 move; requires confirmed M5 and aligned H1/M15, ATR $2.5–$8, spread ≤$0.60.
WAIT: News near, unverified news, missing confirmation, choppy conditions unsuitable for quick scalps, unusually wide spread, or extreme ATR.
